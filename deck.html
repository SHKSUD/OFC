The farmer sells blind.

He doesn't know the real price. The middleman is his only reference.

NERAMAC publishes nothing he can access at village level. Government price boards sit in district towns — not in Karbi Anglong or East Khasi Hills. So the farmer takes whatever the middleman offers.
